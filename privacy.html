Privacy policy — Cottonwood Creek Tales

Last updated: October 20, 2026.

The "Cottonwood Creek Tales" application is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Cottonwood Creek Tales to upload its own videos, edit their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and read the channel's statistics.

Data it accesses

Through the YouTube API Services and with the owner's permission, the application accesses the owner's channel data (videos, metadata, captions, comments and YouTube Analytics statistics). It does not access data from other users or other channels.

Use, storage and sharing

The data is used only to manage the channel itself. The access token is stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, transferred or shared with third parties.

This application uses the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account security settings.
